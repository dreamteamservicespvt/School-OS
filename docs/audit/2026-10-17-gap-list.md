# School OS: whole-platform audit and gap list

17 October 2026. Branch `claude/project-thread-cesreq`.

How this was done: every module's feature list in `docs/blueprint/modules/` was checked against the code (`src/lib`, `src/app`, `prisma/schema`), the open defects in `qa/results/` were re-checked, and the findings were compared with the best school software in India and abroad (Teachmint, Entab, Fedena, Edunext, MyClassboard, Skolaro, PowerSchool, Toddle, Arbor, SchoolStatus, Brightwheel, Veracross and others) and with the earlier 16-product demo study. Features were only adopted where they make School OS simpler or safer for a school; nothing under "Not included" was added.

## The short answer

School OS is unusually complete. All 20 modules are built, a search for unfinished pages or TODOs finds nothing real, and the full test suite passes. The real gaps were in three places:

1. **Prediction.** Early Warnings only looked back ("this student is below 75%"). Leading products tell management what is likely to happen next. School OS had no forward-looking signals for fees, seats, staff cover or cash.
2. **Moving in from old software.** Two money bugs (past receipts did not reduce dues; old dues could be brought in twice), an unsafe undo, and heading matching that failed on small typos and on common Indian software exports.
3. **A handful of open QA defects** from the Chaitanya audit.

## Still to do, in priority order

### P0: needed before the first school goes live

These are not code. Nothing can go live without them.

1. **Hosting.** Choose the hosting company; set up the database with backups, file storage and email in India.
2. **Business accounts.** WhatsApp Business (Cloud API), MSG91 for SMS with DLT templates registered, a Razorpay account, and DREAM TEAM's GST details for invoices. Until these exist, School OS runs messages in a safe test mode and online fee payment is off.
3. **Three product decisions** from the QA report: late marks entry, re-opened exams, and the duplicate-sibling rule.

### P1: worth building next

1. **Support Access and restore in the Super Admin console.** A DREAM TEAM person cannot yet look at a school's screen with the school's permission, or restore an archived record from the console. Needed for real support.
2. **Dashboard speed (PLATFORM-13).** The leader home runs over 60 widgets in three groups, one after another, and shares one memo across three transactions. Run each group's widgets together and keep memoised queries inside their own transaction.
3. **Pages that do not exist return 200 (PLATFORM-17).** Under a `loading.tsx` the not-found page streams with status 200. Search engines and monitoring see it as a real page.
4. **One importer, not three.** Student, fee and staff imports each have their own upload and column-matching screens, and there are two column matchers. Move them all onto the Switching to School OS engine, so typo-tolerant matching and value tidying work everywhere.
5. **One student search.** There are seven separate student-search functions with different rules, and none forgives a typo. Make one shared function (name, admission number, parent name or phone, with one letter wrong allowed) and use it everywhere.
6. **Attendance is flagged twice.** Attendance Flag (attendance module) and Early Warning (Reports) count the same students in two Dashboard cards. Keep the Attendance Flag as the record and let Early Warnings read it.
7. **Promise to pay.** When the accountant calls a family, record "will pay on 25 October": reminders pause until then and the family comes back to the call list if the date passes. Leading fee tools do this; it also makes the fee forecast more honest.
8. **Absence reasons.** Capture a reason for each absence (sick, travel, family function, fees dispute, unknown). Reasons drive the right action and make the leaving warning more accurate.

### P2: useful, not urgent

1. **Photos of paper registers.** Take photos of the admission or fee register; AI builds the table and the same import preview shows it, with every unsure cell highlighted and names, dates of birth, phones and amounts always needing a person's tick. Needs an AI service account.
2. **Old Excel (.xls) files.** Only .xlsx and .csv are read today; many old Indian school packages still export .xls.
3. **Library loans and unpaid fines on moving in.** Only the catalogue can be imported, so a school switching mid-year loses which books are out.
4. **Forecast accuracy.** Once a month, compare last month's fee forecast with what came in, so the accountant learns how far to trust it.
5. **Cash for the next 8 weeks.** Extend the cash warning into a weekly view with salaries on the payroll date and known bills.
6. **Remove `Student.medicalNotes`.** A health field that nothing reads or writes; Safety and Health is "Not included". Needs a migration that keeps existing data safe (archive the values first).
7. **Transport map.** The live map is a drawn sketch without real roads. Transport is built as an optional module, but the decision is that it is not in the first version schools buy, so this waits.

## Looked at and not adopted

- **A "flight risk" score for named staff.** High harm, very little data, and sensitive in Indian private schools. Substitution load is shown instead.
- **Black-box risk scores.** Every signal is a plain rule with its numbers shown. Research on statewide dropout models (Wisconsin DEWS) found them wrong most of the time and unfair to some groups.
- **Face counting from class photos for attendance.** Privacy risk with children's faces; one-tap attendance is already fast.
- **AI grading and worksheet generation.** Teaching content, not school operations.
- **Tuition insurance and paying vendors through School OS.** Need outside partners; not for version 1.

# Second round: a deeper check, 17 October 2026 (evening)

How this was done: 13 separate read-only checks, one per area, compared every line of each module's feature list in `docs/blueprint/` with the code. Six research passes looked at AI school products (PowerSchool, Panorama, Infinite Campus, Teachmint, Toddle and others), Indian school software (Entab, Fedena, MyClassboard, Edunext, Skolaro and others), global school systems (Arbor, Veracross, Compass, ManageBac and others), the best data-import tools (Flatfile, OneSchema, Dromo and others), Indian rules (DPDP Rules 2025, APAAR, UDISE+, DLT, GST) and fee, HR and payroll practice. The findings were merged into one list. Then every P0 and P1 item was checked again against the code by a separate reader, told to look for reasons the item was wrong, before anything was built.

## What this round found

The first round found School OS complete on paper. This round found that **many finished screens break on real school data**. Examples: a fee structure republished mid-year bills months already paid; online payments can be taken and never posted; messages fail without telling anyone when WhatsApp is off; a guardian barred by a custody order still sees the child on Parent Link; one bad row stops a whole student import; loss-of-pay leave is never deducted from salary; four of the eight AI switches have nothing behind them. These matter more than new features, so they come first.

Status: "Building" means a builder is on it now. Items from the first round's P1 list (dashboard speed, not-found pages, one student search, attendance flagged twice, promise to pay, absence reasons) are being built now and are not repeated here.

## Looked at and not adopted (second round)

- **Machine-learning dropout or 'at risk' score using caste/category, religion, RTE/EWS status, gender or income (Wisconsin DEWS, EAB, Nevada GRAD).** Black-box and unfair; Wisconsin DEWS was wrong about three-quarters of the time. School OS keeps plain rules with numbers shown, no fixed-trait inputs (00-global §2.1).
- **Fully autonomous admissions agent that contacts families or decides on its own (Blackbaud Admissions Agent full mode) and AI auto-reply to enquiries without pause.** AI may only suggest and never act without a person's yes; any enquiry reply must be drafted for a counsellor to send.
- **Teacher 'flight risk' score for named staff.** High harm, little data, sensitive in Indian private schools; substitution load and school-level retention are shown instead.
- **Health, infirmary, immunisation and sick-bay records (Vidyalaya, MyClassboard, Compass Chronicle sick bay).** Safety & Health is under 'Not included'. Student.medicalNotes should be archived, not extended.
- **Full parent app, parent login, parent chat with teachers, parent leave requests from an app.** 'Full parent app or parent login' is not included; parents use passwordless Parent Link until after Stage 4.
- **Admission form embedded on the school's website or social pages, website builder (Skolaro, Eduflex).** School Website including an online admission form on a website is not included; the standalone /apply link already exists.
- **Family-facing AI chatbot answering parents (PowerBuddy for Engagement, ParentSquare assistant).** Needs a parent channel beyond Parent Link and risks confidently wrong fee or date answers about children; revisit with the parent app.
- **Automatically blocking report cards, hall tickets, exams or classes for unpaid fees, or naming defaulters publicly.** Punitive and the subject of child-rights and court action in India; holds stay a Principal decision and messages stay private.
- **Pushing no-cost EMI fee financing or making autopay a condition of attendance (Jodo, GrayQuest, TeachPay Credit).** Costs schools ~7% plus subvention and pressures families; not core operations, could be an opt-in partner link later.
- **AI question papers, worksheets and quiz generators, classroom AI panels (Teachmint EduAI, MyClassboard Examcell AI, Gemini/Copilot teacher tools).** Free in Google and Microsoft; teaching content, not school operations. Hide the 'ai-question-papers' switch rather than build it.
- **Always-on AI scanning of messages (ClassDojo CSAM/keyword scanning, Gaggle-style surveillance).** High false alarms and privacy harm; beyond the DPDP education and safety purpose.
- **Live CCTV feeds for parents.** Privacy risk to other children and staff, not required.
- **Storing fingerprint or face templates and child face images in the cloud; face counting from class photos.** Data minimisation under DPDP; devices keep templates, School OS keeps punch logs only.
- **Pay-per-use AI credits charged to schools (Classe365).** Unpredictable cost discourages consistent use; AI usage is metered internally and included per plan.
- **Asking schools for UDISE+, CBSE or state-portal passwords to upload or scrape on their behalf.** Credential risk and breaks portal terms; School OS prepares exact upload files instead.
- **Seating-plan registers with in-lesson behaviour points (Arbor My Classroom).** Adds complexity without helping the Indian daily register, which one-tap Take Attendance already covers.
- **Vendor-only closed migration done by the vendor team with no school-visible steps (Teachmint, Entab).** School OS keeps self-serve, checkable import with preview, reconciliation and undo; assisted mode uses the same screens via Support Access.
- **Share the App link.** Removed from the menu per README 'Not included'.
## Decisions taken for the AI work

- **Predictions stay plain rules on the school's own records**, with the numbers shown, labelled "Suggestion", and nothing changing until a person decides. No black-box scores and no fixed traits (caste, religion, gender, income, RTE status).
- **One AI service layer (G28) before any feature that needs a language model** (Ask in plain words, translation, enquiry reply drafts, photos of registers). It is off until DREAM TEAM sets a key and the school switches the feature on. Restricted fields (Aadhaar, salary, counselling notes) are never sent. Every call is logged. Every feature still works without it, using today's rule-based behaviour.
- **AI question papers are not built.** The switch is hidden (G24): free tools already do this, and it is teaching content, not running the school.
