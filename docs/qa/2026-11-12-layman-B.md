## Problems

### LB1 (High) A concession far bigger than the fee is accepted and sent for approval
- Steps: Accountant > Fee Management > Concessions & Refunds > New concession > find Sravya Joshi (Class 3 B, ₹16,899 due, tuition ₹32,000 a year) > Type Discount, Period "Rest of this year", Amount ₹ 50000, Fee head Tuition fee, reason > Apply concession.
- What happened: it was saved as "Discount ₹50,000 · Waiting for Principal". It shows as ₹50,000 in the list and in the Discount and concession report. The Principal's sheet says "Amount or % ₹50,000" with a small "About ₹8,000" line. Nothing tells the approver that the ₹50,000 is more than the student's whole tuition. Reproduced twice (two requests were made).
- What should happen: refuse it on the form, the way New refund does ("Maximum refund ₹101"), e.g. "Not more than ₹8,000 still to pay on the ticked heads this period". A percentage above 100 should be refused the same way (not checked: the server was too slow for that run).
- Clean-up: the Principal declined both LB requests with a reason. They stay on record as "Rejected".
- Screenshots: `qa-output/layman-b/conc1-amt-50000.png` (form), `qa-output/layman-b/conc-50000-principal.png` (approver view); compare `qa-output/layman-b/refund-500.png`.
- Likely source: `src/app/(school)/fee-management/concessions/actions.ts` (amount check), `.../concessions/sheets.tsx`.

### LB2 (Medium) Day Book for a range shows ₹0 for every month or day the school lost money
- Steps: Finance Management > Finance Reports > Day Book for a range (2026-27 so far). Also switch to Day with dates 01-10-2026 to 31-10-2026.
- What happened: the bar list above the table shows July ₹0, August ₹0, September ₹0, November ₹0. In the table under it the same months are -₹2,70,092, -₹7,48,244, -₹10,44,093, -₹10,26,608. In the Day view, every October day with only expenses shows ₹0 (the table says -₹3,850, -₹4,200, and so on). A school owner reading the bars thinks those months broke even.
- What should happen: show the loss (a red bar or the minus amount), or label the bars "Surplus only".
- Screenshot: `qa-output/layman-b/rep-finance-day-book-summary.png`.
- Likely source: `src/lib/finance/reports.ts` line 324, `value: r.net > ZERO ? r.net : ZERO`.

### LB3 (Medium) Defaulters and the Outstanding report give different ageing for the same overdue money
- Steps: open Fee Management > Defaulters, then Fee Reports > Outstanding report.
- What happened: both total ₹89,64,572 overdue. Defaulters says 0 to 30 days ₹0, 31 to 60 days ₹0, 61 to 90 days ₹0, over 90 days ₹89,64,572 (319 students). The Outstanding report says 0 to 30 days ₹1,63,800, 31 to 60 days ₹42,78,570, 61 to 90 days ₹1,34,252, over 90 days ₹43,87,950. The Fee Dashboard says "319 over 30 days". Defaulters puts each student's whole overdue into the group of their oldest unpaid row; the report ages each row by its own due date. A layman sees two answers to "how much is over 90 days late".
- What should happen: one rule on both screens, or say on Defaulters "grouped by the oldest unpaid due date".
- Screenshots: `qa-output/layman-b/accountant_fee-management_defaulters.png`, `qa-output/layman-b/rep-fee-outstanding.png`.
- Likely source: `src/lib/fees/defaulters.ts` and `src/lib/fees/reports.ts` (outstanding).

### LB4 (Medium) Finance "Fees" income does not match Fee Reports (application fees are counted only in Finance)
- Steps: Finance Reports > Income and expense (or Profit and loss, or Day Book for a range) and Fee Management > Fee Reports > Collection report, same year.
- What happened: Finance shows Fees ₹48,15,000 for the year and ₹45,250 for October. Fee Reports show ₹48,05,750 and ₹36,000 for October (30 payments). The ₹9,250 difference is the admission application fees (receipts SPS/AF/...) posted on 31-10-2026, which the Day Book lists under "Other fee types". The Fee Dashboard ("Collected this year ₹48.06 L") and every Fee Report leave them out, and no screen explains the gap.
- What should happen: show application fees as their own income line ("Application fees") in Finance, or add them to a Fee Report, so the two totals can be matched.
- Screenshots: `qa-output/layman-b/daybook-31-10.png`, `qa-output/layman-b/rep-finance-profit-loss.png`, `qa-output/layman-b/rep-fee-collection.png`.
- Likely source: `src/lib/finance/reports.ts` (income heads by system key), `src/lib/fees/reports.ts` (collection).

### LB5 (Medium) Profit and loss "Salaries" does not match Salary History; a staff loan is booked as salary
- Steps: Finance Reports > Profit and loss; Salary Management > Salary History.
- What happened: P&L shows Salaries ₹72,20,048. Salary History shows net paid ₹71,90,048 (gross ₹77,00,964). May 2026 in Income and expense is ₹10,33,199, but the May salary run is ₹10,03,199. The ₹30,000 is Mahesh Yadav's loan paid on 18-05-2026. A loan is money the school gets back, not a salary cost. The P&L also uses net pay, so PF, ESI, PT and TDS taken from salary and the school's own PF/ESI share are missing from expenses.
- What should happen: book advances and loans under their own head ("Staff advances"), and show salary cost as gross pay plus the school's share, or say on the report that it is "net pay paid".
- Screenshot: `qa-output/layman-b/rep-finance-profit-loss.png`, `qa-output/layman-b/accountant_salary-management_history.png`.
- Likely source: `src/lib/finance/reports.ts` / `src/lib/finance/journals.ts` (salary and advance postings).
