Written for: School OS owner and developers. Date: 2026-10-31.

## 1. Run facts

- Skill: Cloudflare security-audit-skill, full audit mode, **standard profile, scoped to the 9 units run-1 deferred. This is a partial pass, not complete coverage.**
- Source ref: commit ff255935, branch claude/project-thread-cesreq, dirty worktree (owner's own edits, reviewed as present).
- Prior run: run-1 (quick profile, commit 0b67003b). Its 13 closed units, 2 fixed confirmed records and 4 needs-validation leads are carried in the ledger as out_of_scope, not re-checked here.
- Agents: 15 of a 16 budget. 7 hunters (one wave), 1 post-wave coverage critic, 4 candidate verifiers (Phase 3) and 3 separate fresh record verifiers (Phase 5). No reconnaissance agents (the scope came from run-1's ledger). The final-clean critic was not spent, because coverage was already known to be incomplete.
- Execution: sandboxed-source-and-local-only policy. This Windows host has no OS-enforced sandbox, so **no target code was run; every check is source-only.** Confirmed records say "Not executed (no sandbox)" in their observed result.
- Result: 16 records: 9 confirmed (2 medium, 7 low), 7 needs validation, 0 rejected. Every record passed both an independent Phase 3 verifier and a separate Phase 5 verifier.

## 2. Posture

School-to-school isolation held again. The database unit (row-level security, definer functions, purge_school, background-job claims) closed with no finding, and so did the module on/off unit. Every finding is inside one school and has the same shape as run-1's: a rule that each action, page or route must apply by itself is missing in some of them. Two patterns stand out:

1. **The "One campus" limit.** It lives only in `current.campusId`. Code that treats `see === 'all'` as "the whole school" or never reads campusId lets a campus-limited user see or change other campuses' records. This affects events, certificate detail, the Parent Link preview, government return files, the document-design preview with the privacy person export (one record), and student import undo: 6 confirmed records, all low. A seventh low finding (competency levels) binds a student to the wrong section in the same way.
2. **Who may change the rules.** A Settings reach of "Own" is treated as full Settings change rights, so its holder can make themselves School Admin (medium). The Accountant can edit the approval limits that are meant to restrain the Accountant (medium). Staff & HR can create logins of any role (needs validation).

## 4. Confirmed finding details

### The Accountant can raise or switch off the approval limits that are meant to restrain the Accountant

- Fingerprint: `school-os/money/approval-limits-editable-by-restrained-role`
- Severity: likelihood medium, impact medium, overall **medium**. Confidence medium.
- Source: src/app/(school)/fee-management/settings/actions.ts:89 -> src/app/(school)/fee-management/_lib/action.ts:27 -> src/lib/roles.ts:38 -> src/lib/fees/settings-save.ts:117 -> src/lib/fees/concessions.ts:172
- Lower-trust principal: A school Accountant (malicious or careless insider) who wants to give a large fee concession or counter discount, cancel an older receipt, or approve and pay a large expense without the Principal's approval.
- Reproduction (native interface, not executed): Sign in as a dummy-school user who holds only the ready Accountant role.; Open Fee Settings, press Change on the Payments card, enter a very high concession limit and untick cancel-past approval, and save.; Give a concession above the old limit: it is applied at once with status APPROVED and decidedById = the same user, and no Approval row is created.; Cancel a receipt dated before today: it is cancelled at once instead of waiting for approval.; In Finance Settings, raise 'Approved at once up to', then record and pay a large expense: it is approved by the requester and posted to the Day Book.
- Conditions: authentication_level A signed-in school user who completed two-step sign-in (the ready Accountant role requires it). authorization_role The ready Accountant role (Full Fee and Finance Management, no Settings), or any custom role with Full Fee Management (change reach 'all') or Full Finance Management but no Settings. system_configuration Default role templates. The school has not narrowed the Accountant's Fee or Finance Management access.
- Result: Not executed (no sandbox). Source trace establishes: paymentsAction, counterAction (fee settings actions.ts lines 89, 96) and billsAction (finance settings actions.ts line 27) are reachable with only Fee Management 'edit' plus change 'all', or Finance Management 'edit', both of which the ready Accountant holds (roles.ts line 38). savePayments, saveCounter and saveFinanceSettings do no Settings or role check. giveConcession (concessions.ts:172), counter discounts and waivers (collect.ts:128), cancelPayment (cancel.ts:53) and submitExpense (expenses.ts:127) read these saved values as the only approval gate.
- Impact: Removes the Principal's maker-checker control over fee concessions, counter discounts and waivers, cancellation of older receipts, and expense payouts inside one school. The changes are audit-logged and visible on the settings pages, which limits concealment but not the loss. There is no cross-school effect.
- Priority: Needs a malicious or careless insider in the Accountant role, but it uses a normal settings form that the page offers to that role, and no technical skill is needed.
- Smallest fix: Gate saving of approval limits and counter permissions on the Settings module (requirePermission(current.permissions, 'settings', 'edit'), or a Principal/School Admin check), and make those fields read-only on Fee and Finance Settings, as the blueprint says. Better still, move the thresholds into the Forms & Approvals approval rules. Add a regression test that an Accountant calling paymentsAction, counterAction or billsAction is refused and that the stored limits and counter map are unchanged.

## 6. Hardening notes (not findings)

- SECURITY DEFINER functions (purge_school, claim_background_jobs, active_school_ids, find_parent_link, verify_certificate, admission_school_by_code, prune_*) use 'SET search_path = public' without an explicit trailing pg_temp, so pg_temp is searched first for relation names. purge_school also uses unqualified names and format('DELETE FROM %I ...'), and the Day Book trigger (day_book_is_append_only) reads unqualified 'school'. scripts/setup-db.sh does not REVOKE TEMPORARY ON DATABASE FROM PUBLIC. Anyone able to run SQL as school_os_app could shadow console_school/school/platform_invoice with temp tables and make purge_school delete a real school's payments and Day Book, defeating the no-DELETE rule. No SQL-injection path into the app role was found, so this is defence in depth only. Fix: SET search_path = pg_catalog, public, pg_temp (or schema-qualify every name, including format('DELETE FROM public.%I')), and REVOKE TEMPORARY ON DATABASE <db> FROM PUBLIC.
- purge_school's 'login used only in this school' check (latest body in prisma/schema/migrations/20261017000000_demo_schools_deletable/migration.sql, login loop) has been dead since 20261022130000_own_access_only_before_school. With app.school_id set to the target, school_access_read hides other schools' access rows, so NOT EXISTS (... school_id <> target) is always true and every login with access to the target is queued for deletion. Today the FK school_access_user_login_id_fkey (ON DELETE RESTRICT; RI checks ignore RLS) raises restrict_violation, which rolls back that login's whole sub-block (session, two_step_challenge, trusted_device, user_login deletes), so shared logins survive. Fix: clear app.school_id (set it to '') before the loop, or check without RLS dependence. Add a test that purges a school whose admin also works in another school and asserts that login, its sessions and its trusted devices remain.
- school.is_demo relaxes purge_school (students/payments/invoices) and the Day Book append-only trigger, but it lives on the tenant-writable 'school' row (school_update policy + table-wide UPDATE grant to school_os_app). Today only prisma/seed.ts writes it. A future school-side mass-assignment bug would let a school flag itself as demo. The migration also flags any database school matching ('SPS','Sunrise Public School') or ('GVS','Green Valley School'). Fix: move the flag to console_school, or REVOKE UPDATE (is_demo) ON school FROM school_os_app.
- app_in_console() is a plain GUC check that school_os_app can set itself. With it on, console_* tables, platform_invoice and support_access_request show every school's rows (no per-school predicate), and withConsoleSchool also shows all records of the named school. School-side callers (src/lib/console/school-billing.ts, src/lib/console/support.ts) rely on code-level forSchoolId filters, all present today. Consider adding AND (app_school_id() IS NULL OR for_school_id = app_school_id()) to the console-only policies for per-school console tables.
- Foreign keys reference id alone, not (school_id, id), and RI checks ignore RLS. A school row can therefore point at another school's record UUID whenever app code accepts an id without first loading it under withSchool. Unique indexes keyed on such ids (e.g. application_student_id_key, alumni_record_student_id_key, bed_allocation_one_active_per_student, student_fee_due(student_id, installment_id)) could then block the other school. This needs the other school's random v4 UUID. Composite (school_id, id) FKs would close it at the database.
- tests/isolation.test.ts 'every school table has its walls' (lines 298-330) accepts any policy count > 0, so a table whose only policy is a *_purge DELETE policy would pass. That is fail-closed for reads, but the test does not prove a same-school WITH CHECK exists. Assert a policy with polcmd='*' whose qual is school_id = app_school_id().
- src/app/(school)/communication/queries/actions.ts logQueryAction: fromId (guardian/student/staff) and aboutStudentId are taken from the form and not bound to the sender's scope; an own-reach teacher can attach any guardian UUID, which copies that guardian's name and phone into a query the teacher can read and sends the ticket number to that guardian. Bind them with the communication scope (campus/sections).

- src/app/(school)/staff-hr-management/[staffId]/actions.ts newSignInLink returns the invite link to the caller even when a message was sent to the person; consider showing it only when no channel delivered it, and never for logins holding roles the caller lacks.
