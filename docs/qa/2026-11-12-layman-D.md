## LD1. Staff leave: after the form refuses, the From date quietly jumps back to today; the leave is then saved for the wrong days (High)

- **Steps (HR, Kavitha Reddy):** Staff & HR → Staff Leave → Apply for leave. Staff member: LD-రమేష్ Ramesh 1345. Leave type: Casual. From 20-11-2026, To 18-11-2026 (a mistake), reason typed. Press Apply.
- **What happened:** the message "To date is before the from date" is right, but **both** date boxes are reset to today (12-11-2026). Staff member, leave type and reason stay. A person fixes only the To date (21-11-2026) and presses Apply: the leave is saved as **12-11-2026 to 21-11-2026, 8 days**, not 20-11 to 21-11 (2 days). The balance shows "8 waiting".
- **Seen twice:** first run saved the 8-day leave (still in the database, see "Data left"); second run read the boxes right after the refusal: before `20-11-2026 / 18-11-2026`, after `12-11-2026 / 12-11-2026`.
- **Should happen:** after a refusal, every box keeps what the person typed, dates included.
- **Screenshot:** `qa-output/layman-d/leave2-after-refused.png`, `qa-output/layman-d/leave-to-before-from.png`
- **Likely source:** `src/app/(school)/staff-hr-management/leave/leave-forms.tsx` lines 50-51 (`defaultValue={f.val("from", today)}`) with the shared date box (the combobox date field with a hidden `name="from"` input) not taking the returned value after a refused submit. Check other forms that use the same date field inside a `FormDialog`: they may lose dates the same way.
