# School OS: whole-platform audit and gap list

17 October 2026. Branch `claude/project-thread-cesreq`.

How this was done: every module's feature list in `docs/blueprint/modules/` was checked against the code (`src/lib`, `src/app`, `prisma/schema`), the open defects in `qa/results/` were re-checked, and the findings were compared with the best school software in India and abroad (Teachmint, Entab, Fedena, Edunext, MyClassboard, Skolaro, PowerSchool, Toddle, Arbor, SchoolStatus, Brightwheel, Veracross and others) and with the earlier 16-product demo study. Features were only adopted where they make School OS simpler or safer for a school; nothing under "Not included" was added.

## The short answer

School OS is unusually complete. All 20 modules are built, a search for unfinished pages or TODOs finds nothing real, and the full test suite passes. The real gaps were in three places:

1. **Prediction.** Early Warnings only looked back ("this student is below 75%"). Leading products tell management what is likely to happen next. School OS had no forward-looking signals for fees, seats, staff cover or cash.
2. **Moving in from old software.** Two money bugs (past receipts did not reduce dues; old dues could be brought in twice), an unsafe undo, and heading matching that failed on small typos and on common Indian software exports.
3. **A handful of open QA defects** from the Chaitanya audit.

## Still to do, in priority order

### P0: needed before the first school goes live

These are not code. Nothing can go live without them.

1. **Hosting.** Choose the hosting company; set up the database with backups, file storage and email in India.
2. **Business accounts.** WhatsApp Business (Cloud API), MSG91 for SMS with DLT templates registered, a Razorpay account, and DREAM TEAM's GST details for invoices. Until these exist, School OS runs messages in a safe test mode and online fee payment is off.
3. **Three product decisions** from the QA report: late marks entry, re-opened exams, and the duplicate-sibling rule.

### P1: worth building next

1. **Support Access and restore in the Super Admin console.** A DREAM TEAM person cannot yet look at a school's screen with the school's permission, or restore an archived record from the console. Needed for real support.
2. **Dashboard speed (PLATFORM-13).** The leader home runs over 60 widgets in three groups, one after another, and shares one memo across three transactions. Run each group's widgets together and keep memoised queries inside their own transaction.
3. **Pages that do not exist return 200 (PLATFORM-17).** Under a `loading.tsx` the not-found page streams with status 200. Search engines and monitoring see it as a real page.
4. **One importer, not three.** Student, fee and staff imports each have their own upload and column-matching screens, and there are two column matchers. Move them all onto the Switching to School OS engine, so typo-tolerant matching and value tidying work everywhere.
5. **One student search.** There are seven separate student-search functions with different rules, and none forgives a typo. Make one shared function (name, admission number, parent name or phone, with one letter wrong allowed) and use it everywhere.
6. **Attendance is flagged twice.** Attendance Flag (attendance module) and Early Warning (Reports) count the same students in two Dashboard cards. Keep the Attendance Flag as the record and let Early Warnings read it.
7. **Promise to pay.** When the accountant calls a family, record "will pay on 25 October": reminders pause until then and the family comes back to the call list if the date passes. Leading fee tools do this; it also makes the fee forecast more honest.
8. **Absence reasons.** Capture a reason for each absence (sick, travel, family function, fees dispute, unknown). Reasons drive the right action and make the leaving warning more accurate.

### P2: useful, not urgent

1. **Photos of paper registers.** Take photos of the admission or fee register; AI builds the table and the same import preview shows it, with every unsure cell highlighted and names, dates of birth, phones and amounts always needing a person's tick. Needs an AI service account.
2. **Old Excel (.xls) files.** Only .xlsx and .csv are read today; many old Indian school packages still export .xls.
3. **Library loans and unpaid fines on moving in.** Only the catalogue can be imported, so a school switching mid-year loses which books are out.
4. **Forecast accuracy.** Once a month, compare last month's fee forecast with what came in, so the accountant learns how far to trust it.
5. **Cash for the next 8 weeks.** Extend the cash warning into a weekly view with salaries on the payroll date and known bills.
6. **Remove `Student.medicalNotes`.** A health field that nothing reads or writes; Safety and Health is "Not included". Needs a migration that keeps existing data safe (archive the values first).
7. **Transport map.** The live map is a drawn sketch without real roads. Transport is built as an optional module, but the decision is that it is not in the first version schools buy, so this waits.

## Looked at and not adopted

- **A "flight risk" score for named staff.** High harm, very little data, and sensitive in Indian private schools. Substitution load is shown instead.
- **Black-box risk scores.** Every signal is a plain rule with its numbers shown. Research on statewide dropout models (Wisconsin DEWS) found them wrong most of the time and unfair to some groups.
- **Face counting from class photos for attendance.** Privacy risk with children's faces; one-tap attendance is already fast.
- **AI grading and worksheet generation.** Teaching content, not school operations.
- **Tuition insurance and paying vendors through School OS.** Need outside partners; not for version 1.
