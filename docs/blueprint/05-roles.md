# 05. Roles: who sees what

This page shows which roles can use which of the 20 modules. The first table has the ten ready roles every school starts with. The second table has the role templates the school can switch on (decided 9 October 2026). The school can change any of them, copy them, or make new roles in Settings, Users & Permissions. A person can have more than one role, and the menu shows only the modules their roles allow.

## What the marks mean

| Mark | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Full** | Can see and do everything in the module, within what Settings, Forms & Approvals allows. |
| **View** | Can see, but not add or change. The notes below say where View is only part of a module. |
| **Own** | Only their own classes or records. For a teacher, the classes and subjects given to them in Teacher Allocation. For everyone, their own leave, salary slips and profile. In the role templates, Own can also mean the one part of a module named in the notes. |
| **-** | None. The module is not in their menu. |

## Notes for each role

**Principal.** Everything. A school owner who wants to see everything is given this role or School Admin. The Dashboard, Approvals and Reports come first. Approves leave, discounts, refunds, large expenses, admissions, certificates, the month's salary and results, as set in Settings, Forms & Approvals.

**School Admin.** The person who sets up and runs the software for the school, usually the office head. Gets everything, including Settings, Users & Permissions. A school can narrow this role, for example to keep salary with the accountant and principal only.

**Accountant.** Fee Management, Finance Management, Salary Management and money reports.
- Student Management: View, only to find a student and see their details for fees. No marks, no behaviour or counselling notes.
- Communication: Own, for fee reminders, receipts and payment links to parents.
- Inventory Management: View, to see purchases and vendor bills that reach Finance Management.
- Events & Calendar: View, to see the School Calendar and event payments.
- Reports & Analytics: View, money reports only.
- Staff & HR Management: Own, for their own profile and leave.

**Teacher.** Only their own classes and subjects: Take Attendance, Time Table, Homework, Marks Entry, lesson plans and those students' profiles.
- Academic Management: their own Time Table, syllabus progress and lesson plans.
- Student Management: profiles of the students they teach. Can report a behaviour incident for those students.
- Communication: messages to parents of the students they teach.
- Events & Calendar: sees the School Calendar; manages their own PTM slots and notes.
- Reports & Analytics: reports for their own classes.
- Staff & HR Management and Salary Management: own leave and own salary slips.

**Class Teacher.** Everything a Teacher has, plus their own section as a whole:
- the section's attendance register and attendance corrections,
- student leave for the section, recording it and deciding it where Forms & Approvals allows,
- every student profile in the section,
- report card remarks for the section,
- checking the promotion list for the section at year end.

**Receptionist / Front Office.** Front Office in full.
- Admission Management: Own. Adds new enquiries and works on the ones they own.
- Student Management: View, only to search for a student for pickup and see the approved pickup list. Makes the Pickup Gate Pass in Front Office.
- Communication: Own, messages to parents.
- Events & Calendar: View, to answer callers about holidays and events.

**Transport In-charge.** Transport Management only: live buses, routes, students on each bus, trips and driver papers.
- Communication: Own, messages to parents of a route, including emergency messages.

**Librarian.** Library Management only: books, issue and return, members and fines.
- Student Management: View, only to search for a student to issue a book.
- The Librarian's search covers both students and staff members (name, photo, class or department only), so books can be issued to staff too. It shows nothing else from Staff & HR Management.

**Hostel Warden.** Hostel Management in full: rooms, beds, roll call, outings, visitors and mess. Approves Hostel Outing Gate Passes.
- Student Management: Own, profiles of the boarders in their hostel.

**Store Keeper.** Inventory Management in full: items, stock, issue, purchases, assets and maintenance requests.

**Every role with a login also gets:**
- Dashboard: their own home screen, with only the numbers and work that belong to them.
- Staff & HR Management: own profile, own leave and own documents.
- Salary Management: own salary slips.
- Events & Calendar: the School Calendar, unless the school turns it off for a role.

The Driver and Student templates below are the exceptions: they get only what their own notes say.

**Vice Principal.** Runs the school day with the Principal: academics, attendance, exams, homework and events in full, and sees everything else. Approves what Settings, Forms & Approvals gives this role, such as student and staff leave. No Settings.

**Academic Head.** Teaching and exams for the whole school: classes, subjects, Time Table, substitutions, syllabus, datesheets, results and report cards.
- Student Management and Attendance Management: View, to follow progress across classes.
- Reports & Analytics: View, academic reports only.

**HR.** Staff & HR Management in full: staff records, documents, leave and leave balances, recruitment and exits.
- Attendance Management: Own, meaning staff attendance only. No student attendance.
- Salary Management: View, to check leave and attendance reaching the salary. The Accountant runs the payroll. A school where HR runs the payroll can change this to Full.
- Reports & Analytics: View, staff reports only.

**Office Staff.** The office work the workflows give it:
- Student Management: Full, including records, imports, certificates, ID cards and recording a student leaving. Counselling notes stay hidden.
- Admission Management: Full, for enquiries, applications and documents.
- Academic Management: Own, for arranging substitutions and keeping the Time Table.
- Attendance Management: Own, for recording student leave (parents have no login, so the office records it).
- Examination & Results: Own, for the datesheet and admit cards. No marks entry and no results.
- Communication: Own, messages to parents about the work above.
- Front Office: View, to see visitors and pickups.
- Fee Management: none by default. The school can let office staff collect fees at the counter in Settings, Users & Permissions.

**Exam In-charge.** Examination & Results in full: datesheets, admit cards, seating, marks progress, results, report cards and exam settings.
- Student Management and Academic Management: View, for classes, subjects and students taking exams.
- Reports & Analytics: View, exam reports only.

**Admission Counsellor.** Admission Management in full: enquiries, follow-ups, applications, documents, tests and decisions to send for approval.
- Student Management: View, to check siblings and seats.
- Fee Management: View, only the application and admission fee status.
- Reports & Analytics: View, admission reports only.

**Counsellor.** Student Management: Own, meaning the counselling cases and sessions given to them. This is the only template that can see counselling notes.

**Gate Security.** Front Office: Own, for visitor and gate entries at the gate.
- Student Management: View, only to check a student's approved pickup list and photo.

**Driver.** Uses the small native Android driver app (decided 9 October 2026), not the web menu. Transport Management: Own, meaning today's trips for that driver: start and end a trip, mark stops, mark students boarded or dropped, send the bus's location, and the emergency button. A driver can be on the school's staff or hired: either way there is a Driver record with a login (decided 9 October 2026). A driver who is also on the staff gets their own leave and salary slips the same way as other staff.

**Student.** Only for schools that give students logins. Homework & Learning: Own, meaning their own homework, quizzes, study material and online classes. Nothing else.

## The top bar, for every role

| Top bar item | What each person gets |
|---|---|
| Search | Finds only what their roles allow them to see. |
| Approvals | Shows only the requests they may decide. |
| Notifications | Their own alerts, each opening the right screen. |
| Messages | Their own messages and replies. |
| Profile | Their own details, password, signed-in devices and notification choices. |
| School switcher | Only for people who work in more than one school or campus. |
| Academic year | Everyone. Past years open read-only. |

## Rules that sit on top of the tables

- **DREAM TEAM is not inside a school.** DREAM TEAM's own people use the Super Admin console and have no role inside a school. They can look inside a school only through a Support Access Request the school allows, for a set time, with restricted details hidden (`07-super-admin.md` §3.4).
- **Sensitive details are hidden** from roles that may not see them, everywhere: on screen, in exports, in Search and in AI answers. Examples: Aadhaar and other ID numbers, salary, private student notes, and counsellor notes, which only allowed staff see.
- **Approvals follow amounts.** Who approves discounts, refunds and expenses, and up to which amount, is set in Settings, Forms & Approvals. Nobody approves their own request.
- **Permissions are checked by the software for every action,** not only hidden from the menu.
- **Optional modules** appear in nobody's menu until the school switches them on in Settings, Modules On/Off.
- **No parent role.** Parents have no login. They get WhatsApp, SMS and email messages and fee payment links.
- **Parent Link is not a role** (decided 9 October 2026). Each guardian gets one personal, secure link with no password, confirmed by a one-time code on a new phone. It shows only that guardian's own children: fees due with a Pay button, receipts, attendance this month, notices, the datesheet, report cards once published, and consent forms. It is read-only apart from paying and giving consent. The school decides what it shows in Communication, Parent Link.
- **One login, several schools.** A person who works in more than one school or campus of a group has one login, with a School Access entry for each school or campus giving their roles and scope there (decided 9 October 2026).
- **Students** get a login only where the school gives students logins. Then they see their own homework, quizzes and classes in Homework & Learning, and nothing else.
