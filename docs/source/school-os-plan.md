# School OS: New Software Plan

Prepared for DREAM TEAM SERVICES, 7 October 2026. For review, one module at a time.

## 1. The idea in one minute

School OS is the complete software for running a school: admissions, students, attendance, fees, exams, staff, salary and everything else, in one place. It keeps every feature of the old software, but every screen is rebuilt around the daily work of a principal, an accountant, a teacher and the office staff. The menu has 20 items with familiar school names, and each person sees only the parts they use. Information is entered once: one fee payment, one admission or one attendance mark updates the receipt, the records, the Day Book and the parent's message by itself. The software stops common mistakes before they happen, tells people what to do next, and asks before anything important that cannot be undone. Where AI helps, it uses only the school's real data, shows its ideas as suggestions, and never acts until a person says yes.

## 2. The menu

Twenty items, in this order, with no group headings. Each person sees only the items their role allows. Pages marked *(optional)* appear only when the school uses them. The last page of each module holds that module's own rules.

**Always at the top of the screen (not in the menu):**

- **Search**: One box to find any student, staff member, receipt or page. Typing "fee Ravi" opens Ravi's fee page.
- **Approvals**: One list of everything waiting for a yes or no: leave, discounts, refunds, expenses, admissions, certificates and corrections.
- **Notifications**: Alerts for the logged-in person, each one opening the right screen.
- **Messages**: The person's own messages and replies.
- **Profile**: Own details, password, signed-in devices and notification choices.
- **School switcher**: Shown only to school groups with more than one school or campus.
- **Academic year**: The year being viewed. Past years open read-only.

### 2.1 Dashboard

The first screen after login. Each person sees today's numbers and the work waiting for them.

- **School Today**: Today's attendance, fee collection, expenses, admissions and buses, each number opening the list behind it.
- **Needs Attention**: What needs action now: students absent 3 days, overdue fees, pending leave, and setup steps not yet done.
- **My Tasks**: Tasks given to staff, with due dates, reminders and status.
- **Today & Upcoming**: Today's substitutions, PTMs and exams, plus coming events and birthdays.

### 2.2 Admission Management

Where the school handles every new admission, from the first enquiry to the day the child joins.

- **Admission Dashboard**: How many enquiries, applications and admissions are at each step this year.
- **Enquiries**: Every enquiry from walk-in, phone, WhatsApp or referral, with follow-up dates, notes and the staff member in charge.
- **Applications**: Admission forms filled at the counter or online, with previous school, marks and application fee.
- **Documents & Verification**: Birth certificate, TC, marksheets and other papers for each applicant, checked against a list per class.
- **Test & Interview** *(optional)*: Entrance test and interview dates, scores and remarks.
- **Admission Decision**: Approve or reject with remarks, keep a waiting list, give the offer letter and confirm the admission.
- **Admission Reports**: Where enquiries come from, how many join, and applications still pending.
- **Admission Settings**: Admission steps, enquiry statuses and sources, papers needed per class, seat and sibling rules, and WhatsApp auto-reply.

### 2.3 Student Management

Every student's full record, from admission to leaving, in one place.

- **Student List**: All students by class and section, with search, Excel import, bulk changes and export.
- **Student Profile**: One screen per student: details, parents, attendance, fees, marks, homework, bus, library, behaviour and documents.
- **Parents & Guardians**: Parents' details and phone numbers, linked to all their children in the school.
- **Behaviour & Counselling**: Discipline incidents, warnings and rewards, plus private counsellor notes seen only by allowed staff.
- **Promotion**: Move a whole class to the next class at year end, after checking results and the list.
- **Leaving & Alumni**: Transfer or withdrawal with fee and library clearance, and records of past students.
- **Certificates & ID Cards**: Print TC, bonafide, study and character certificates, and student ID cards class by class.
- **Student Settings**: Admission number format, required fields, extra fields, student status list and promotion rules.

### 2.4 Academic Management

How teaching is organised: classes, subjects, teachers, the Time Table and the syllabus.

- **Classes & Sections**: Classes, sections, streams, seats per section and class teachers.
- **Subjects**: Subjects for each class, including optional subjects and second languages chosen by students.
- **Teacher Allocation**: Which teacher teaches which subject in which class, and each teacher's workload.
- **Time Table**: Make, check and print the class and teacher Time Table, by hand or automatically, with no clashes.
- **Substitutions**: Arrange a substitute when a teacher is absent, without double booking anyone.
- **Syllabus & Lesson Plans**: Chapters to teach, lesson plans, daily progress and chapters running late.
- **Academic Settings**: Academic year and terms, school timings, periods and breaks, and Time Table rules for teachers and subjects.

### 2.5 Attendance Management

Daily attendance of students and staff, and student leave.

- **Take Attendance**: Mark a whole class present in one tap, then change only the absent and late students.
- **Attendance Register**: Monthly register for each class and student, with corrections and export.
- **Student Leave**: Leave requests for students, approved or rejected with a reason.
- **Staff Attendance**: Daily attendance, late coming, shifts and overtime of teachers and staff.
- **Attendance Reports**: Daily and monthly attendance, percentages and students who are often absent.
- **Devices & QR** *(optional)*: Biometric machines (face, fingerprint, RFID card), QR posters and the campus area for mobile check-in.
- **Attendance Settings**: Attendance types, late rules, the time after which attendance is locked, and alerts to parents.

### 2.6 Fee Management

Collecting fees, keeping each student's fee account and following up on dues.

- **Fee Dashboard**: Today's collection, pending and overdue totals, and Recent Payments as they come in.
- **Collect Fee**: Find the student, see the due, enter the amount and mode, and the receipt is ready. Cash, UPI, card, cheque or online.
- **Student Fee Ledger**: Each student's fees, payments, balance, receipts and fee certificate.
- **Defaulters**: Students with pending fees, how long overdue, and the reminders already sent.
- **Fee Structure**: Class-wise fees for the year, fee types, installments and due dates, including daycare fees.
- **Concessions & Refunds**: Discounts, scholarships, bulk discounts and refunds, each with a reason and approval.
- **Fee Reports**: Collection, dues, discounts, payment modes, and audit and tax reports.
- **Fee Settings**: Late fee rules, reminder schedule, receipt numbering and which payment gateway each class uses.

### 2.7 Finance Management

All money coming in and going out of the school, beyond fees.

- **Day Book**: Every rupee received and spent on a date, filled in automatically.
- **Income**: Money the school receives other than fees.
- **Expenses**: Bills and expenses with a photo of the bill; large amounts go for approval.
- **Cash & Bank**: Cash book and bank book with opening and closing balance.
- **Budget** *(optional)*: Yearly budget by head and department, and how much is used.
- **Accounts** *(optional)*: Journal entries, ledger, payables and receivables, for schools keeping full accounts.
- **Finance Reports**: Income and expense, profit and loss, and department-wise spending.
- **Finance Settings**: Income and expense heads, approval limits, cost centres and Tally export.

### 2.8 Examination & Results

Exams from the Datesheet to the report card.

- **Datesheet**: Create an exam and its subject-wise dates, times, rooms, invigilators and seating.
- **Admit Cards**: Print admit cards for a whole class or one student.
- **Marks Entry**: Teachers enter marks or grades subject by subject, on phone or computer.
- **Results**: Total, percentage, grade and rank worked out automatically, final result across exams, published on a set date.
- **Report Cards**: Board-style report cards with marks, grades, attendance, co-scholastic grades and remarks.
- **Question Papers** *(optional)*: Question bank by chapter and difficulty, and papers made from it.
- **Exam Analysis**: Subject-wise and class-wise results and each student's progress.
- **Exam Settings**: Exam types, mark categories, grade scale (CBSE, ICSE or state board ready-made), pass rules and co-scholastic areas.

### 2.9 Staff & HR Management

Teachers and staff: their records, leave and documents, from joining to leaving.

- **Staff List**: All teachers and staff, with search by name and designation.
- **Staff Profile**: One screen per staff member: details, classes, Time Table, attendance, leave, workload and documents.
- **Add Staff**: Three steps: details, what they can open in the software, and their classes and subjects.
- **Staff Leave**: Apply, approve and cancel leave, with leave balance and a leave calendar.
- **Documents & ID Cards**: Certificates, ID proof and contracts with expiry reminders, experience letters and staff ID cards.
- **Recruitment & Appraisal** *(optional)*: Vacancies, interviews and offers, yearly appraisal, training and promotions.
- **Staff Exit**: Resignation, handover, clearance and final settlement.
- **HR Settings**: Departments, designations, leave types and yearly leave quota.

### 2.10 Salary Management

Monthly salary for all staff, from salary structure to salary slip.

- **Generate Salary**: Monthly salary worked out from attendance and leave, checked, approved and locked.
- **Salary Structures**: Each staff member's earnings and deductions, with past versions kept.
- **Salary Slips**: Salary slips for every staff member; each person can download their own.
- **Advances & Loans** *(optional)*: Salary advances and loans, recovered from monthly pay.
- **Salary History**: Salary paid by month and by staff member, with salary reports.
- **Salary Settings**: Salary components, approval steps and salary slip options.

### 2.11 Communication

Messages, notices and parent queries, by WhatsApp, SMS, email or app.

- **Send Message**: Send to parents, students or staff by class, section, bus route or group, with a preview and cost before sending.
- **Notice Board**: Circulars and notices with attachments, sent to chosen classes or staff, with read tracking.
- **WhatsApp Inbox** *(optional)*: All school WhatsApp chats in one place, each staff member seeing their own.
- **Message History**: Every message sent, with delivered, read and failed status.
- **Feedback & Queries**: Queries and complaints from parents, students and staff, each with a number, an owner and a status.
- **Surveys** *(optional)*: Short surveys such as parent satisfaction, with the answers collected.
- **Message Templates**: Ready message formats in each language, with blanks like student name and due amount.
- **Message Settings**: Automatic messages on or off, birthday wishes, parents who opted out, and query reply time limits.

### 2.12 Transport Management *(can be switched off)*

School buses: routes, vehicles, students on each bus and live tracking.

- **Live Tracking**: Every running bus on a map, with next stop, arrival time and driver.
- **Routes & Stops**: Routes with stops in order, pickup times and stop-wise transport fees.
- **Vehicles & Drivers**: Buses, drivers and conductors, with licence, insurance and fitness expiry reminders, and hired-bus vendors.
- **Student Transport**: Which student uses which route and stop, seat limits, bus passes and missing transport fees.
- **Trips**: Morning and evening trips, the driver app, stops reached and students boarded.
- **Transport Reports**: Trips, timings, usage and fuel and repair costs.
- **Transport Settings**: Speed, route and offline alerts, bus messages to parents, and the GPS device connection.

### 2.13 Library Management *(can be switched off)*

The school library: books, issue and return, and fines.

- **Books**: Book list with author, publisher, copies, accession numbers and barcode labels.
- **Issue & Return**: Issue, return, renew and reserve books in a few taps.
- **Members & Cards**: Students and staff who can borrow, their limits and library cards.
- **Fines & Stock Check**: Late fines, lost or damaged books, and the yearly stock check.
- **Library Reports**: Books issued, overdue, most read and fines collected.
- **Library Settings**: Borrowing limits, loan days and fine rates.

### 2.14 Inventory Management *(can be switched off)*

School stock and assets: what is bought, stored, issued and repaired.

- **Items & Stock**: Every item with stock in hand, stock in and out, and low-stock alerts.
- **Issue Stock**: Give items to staff, departments or classes, and books and uniforms to students.
- **Purchases & Vendors**: Purchase requests, purchase orders, goods received, bills and the vendor list.
- **Assets**: Furniture, computers and equipment with asset tag, location, warranty and repairs.
- **Rooms, Labs & Maintenance**: Rooms, labs and grounds, lab checks and maintenance requests.
- **Inventory Reports**: Stock, purchase and asset reports.
- **Inventory Settings**: Item categories, store locations, reorder levels and approval limits.

### 2.15 Hostel Management *(can be switched off)*

Boarding students: rooms, beds, roll call, outings and mess.

- **Rooms & Beds**: Hostels, floors, rooms and beds, and which beds are free.
- **Allocation**: Give a student a bed, with check-in and check-out.
- **Roll Call & Incidents**: Morning and night roll call, and a record of incidents.
- **Outing & Visitors**: Outing passes with approval and return time, and the hostel visitor log.
- **Mess**: Weekly mess menu, daily head count and mess expenses.
- **Hostel Reports**: Occupancy, roll call and outing reports.
- **Hostel Settings**: Hostel rules, wardens and fees linked to Fee Management.

### 2.16 Front Office

The reception desk: visitors, calls, appointments and student pickup.

- **Reception Desk**: Today's visitors, calls, appointments and walk-in requests on one screen.
- **Visitors**: Register a visitor with photo and pass, tell the staff member, and record time in and out.
- **Student Pickup**: Gate pass for early pickup, given only to parents and guardians on the approved list.
- **Enquiries & Calls**: Walk-in and phone enquiries, which go straight to Admission Management, and the call log.
- **Appointments**: Meetings with the principal or staff, with no double booking.
- **Postal & Deliveries**: Letters and couriers received and sent.

### 2.17 Homework & Learning

Homework, study material and online classes.

- **Homework**: Give homework to a class, section or chosen students in three taps, with attachments and due date.
- **Submissions & Grading**: See who submitted, mark it complete or incorrect, give a score and feedback.
- **Study Material** *(optional)*: Notes, documents, videos and lessons for each class and subject.
- **Quizzes** *(optional)*: Quizzes that mark themselves, for practice and class tests.
- **Discussions** *(optional)*: Class discussion boards for questions and answers.
- **Online Classes** *(optional)*: Online class schedule, meeting link, attendance and recordings.
- **Learning Progress**: Homework done, quiz scores and progress for each student, plus course certificates.
- **Homework Settings**: Submission rules, late marking, grading and who can see which course.

### 2.18 Events & Calendar

The school calendar, events, PTMs, activities and photos.

- **School Calendar**: One calendar for the year: holidays, vacations, exams, events and PTMs.
- **Events**: Plan an event with date, venue and audience; registration, payments and reminders where needed.
- **PTM**: Parent-teacher meeting slots, booking by staff, notes and parent feedback.
- **Co-curricular & Sports**: Activities, competitions, achievements, certificates, houses, clubs and sports teams.
- **Gallery**: Photo and video albums of school events.
- **Daily Activities** *(optional)*: Daily log for play group and pre-primary children: meals, nap, play and activities.
- **Events Settings**: Event types, weekends, house names and calendar sharing to Google or Outlook.

### 2.19 Reports & Analytics

Every report from every module in one place, plus the school's overall picture.

- **All Reports**: Find any report by name or module, filter by year, class or date, and download in Excel or PDF.
- **Analytics**: Trends in students, results, fees, expenses, staff and teacher workload, with numbers you can open.
- **Early Warnings**: Students who may need help, based on attendance, marks and fees, for a person to review.
- **Government Reports**: UDISE+ export, APAAR and PEN details, and board returns in the latest format.
- **Custom & Scheduled Reports** *(optional)*: Build your own report and have it emailed every week or month.

### 2.20 Settings

Only school-wide settings. Each module keeps its own rules on its last page.

- **School Profile**: Name, logo, address, board, campuses, language, and school documents such as affiliation papers.
- **Users & Permissions**: Roles, logins, passwords and what each role can see and approve.
- **Payment Gateways**: Connect Razorpay, PhonePe, Easebuzz and others, and test the connection.
- **SMS, WhatsApp & Email**: Connect SMS, WhatsApp and email, see message credits, and turn each one on or off.
- **Document Designs**: Designs for receipts, certificates, ID cards, admit cards and report cards, with the school's logo and signatures.
- **Forms & Approvals**: Extra fields on forms, custom forms, and who approves what up to which amount.
- **Modules On/Off**: Switch modules on or off, start from a school-type template, and connect Google, Tally, printers and other tools.
- **Data Backup & Security**: Download all school data, see who changed what and when, login history and privacy requests.

## 3. What the software does by itself

1. **Fee paid:** The receipt, the student's balance, the Day Book, the Dashboard and the parent's WhatsApp update together.
2. **Admission approved:** The student record, admission number, class, section, roll number, parent link, fee plan and ID card are created without typing again.
3. **Enquiry at the front desk:** It appears in Admission Enquiries with an owner and a follow-up reminder.
4. **Attendance marked:** Parents of absent students get one message, the register and Dashboard update, and anyone below 75% is flagged to the class teacher and principal.
5. **Teacher leave approved:** The leave balance drops, substitutions are suggested for that teacher's periods, and the leave reaches the salary.
6. **Salary approved:** Salary slips are made, staff are told, and the payment goes into the Day Book.
7. **Fee due date comes:** Reminders go on the set days, the late fee is added after the grace period, and the Defaulters list updates.
8. **Online payment received:** It is posted only after the gateway confirms it, never twice, and matched automatically; failed payments are flagged.
9. **Marks entered:** Total, percentage, grade and rank are worked out; on result day report cards are ready and parents are told.
10. **Datesheet published:** The exams appear in the School Calendar and Time Table, admit cards are ready, and reminders go to parents.
11. **Holiday added:** Attendance is switched off for that day and reports leave it out.
12. **Student given a bus stop:** The stop's transport fee is added to the student's fees, and the bus seat count updates.
13. **Year-end promotion:** Students move to the new class with the new year's fees; last year's record stays unchanged.
14. **Student leaving:** Fee dues and library books are checked before the TC can be printed.
15. **Something about to expire or run out:** Staff documents, driver licences, bus papers, warranties and low stock give a reminder in time.

AI follows three rules everywhere: it uses only the school's real data, it labels suggestions as suggestions, and it asks before any important change that cannot be undone.

## 4. What is different from the old software

- 33 menu items become 20, with clear school names and no group headings.
- User Management is split: students go to Student Management, teachers and staff to Staff & HR, logins to Settings.
- The Dashboard shows today's work and what needs attention; SMS switches, gateway lists and design counts move to Settings.
- The Day Book fills itself from fees, salary and expenses. Nobody types it twice.
- Fee Types are created inside Fee Structure, not on a separate page.
- Payment gateways are set up in one place, Settings, instead of from the Dashboard and the fee screen.
- A wrong fee payment is cancelled with a reason instead of deleted, so the old Deleted Payments Log is no longer needed.
- Leave Management is split: student leave in Attendance, staff leave in Staff & HR, and every approval in one Approvals list.
- Teacher Assignments, Syllabus Plans, Daily Log and Delay Dashboard move from Learning Management to Academic Management.
- Creating a Datesheet creates the exam. Exam Types, Mark Categories and Grade Configuration sit together in Exam Settings.
- CRM / Leads and WhatsApp leads move into Admission Management as Enquiries.
- Message Inbox, Sent Box and Message Logs become one Message History with delivery status.
- Transport has one simple screen instead of a Modern view and a Classic view.
- Quick Filter and Favorites are replaced by one Search box at the top.
- Each module keeps its own rules on its last page, such as Fee Settings and Exam Settings, so Settings stays short.

## 5. Who sees what

- **Principal:** Everything, with the Dashboard, Approvals and Reports first; approves leave, discounts, refunds and results.
- **Accountant:** Fee Management, Finance Management, Salary Management and money reports; no marks or private student notes.
- **Teacher:** Only their own classes: Take Attendance, Time Table, Homework, Marks Entry, lesson plans and those students' profiles, plus their own leave and salary slips.
- **Receptionist / Front Office:** Front Office, new enquiries in Admission Management, student search for pickup, and messages to parents.
- **Transport in-charge:** Transport Management only: live buses, routes, students on each bus, trips and driver papers, plus messages to parents of a route.
- **Librarian:** Library Management only: books, issue and return, members and fines, with a student search.

The school can change any of these, or make new roles such as Vice Principal or Warden, in Settings, Users & Permissions.

## 6. Works for 100 different schools

Schools differ by settings and modules switched on, never by special programming.

- **Board:** CBSE, ICSE, state board, IB, Cambridge or the school's own; the board sets grading, report card and terms. Two boards in one school also work.
- **Grading:** Marks, percentage, grades only, GPA or CGPA; ranks on or off; the school's own grade scale and pass marks.
- **Terms and exams:** Yearly, two terms or semesters, with the school's own exams such as unit tests, FA, SA, half yearly and annual.
- **Fee plans:** Monthly, quarterly, half-yearly, term or yearly, with installments, category-wise fees, scholarships, late fees and daycare billing.
- **Classes and sections:** Play group to class 12 and junior college, any number of sections, streams, optional subjects and languages.
- **Day or boarding:** Day school, boarding or both; hostel and mess appear only where needed.
- **Attendance method:** Once a day or every period; register, mobile, QR, face, fingerprint or RFID card.
- **Admission steps:** With or without entrance test, interview, waiting list, sibling priority or quota seats.
- **Languages:** Screens and messages in English, Hindi, Telugu, Tamil, Kannada, Malayalam, Marathi and Bengali.
- **Modules and campuses:** Each module on or off, a ready start for each school type, and one school or a group of campuses each with its own fees and branding.

## 7. Build order, one by one

Each module is designed, reviewed by you, then built, before the next one starts.

### Stage 1: Foundation

1. **Settings:** School profile, users and roles are needed by every other screen.
2. **Academic Management:** The academic year, classes, sections and subjects must exist before anyone is placed in them.
3. **Staff & HR Management:** Add Staff needs classes and subjects; the Time Table and class teachers need staff.
4. **Student Management:** Students and parents are the base for fees, attendance and exams.

### Stage 2: Daily school work (first sellable version)

5. **Communication:** Fee, attendance and admission messages all go out through it.
6. **Admission Management:** New students come in here and become students automatically.
7. **Fee Management:** The most used module in an Indian school office; needs students and classes.
8. **Finance Management:** The Day Book fills from fee payments, so it follows fees.
9. **Attendance Management:** Needs students, staff and the holiday list; the School Calendar page is built here too.
10. **Dashboard:** Shows numbers from all of the above, so it comes last in this stage.

After Stage 2 the school can run admissions, students, attendance, fees, Day Book and messages. This is the first version a school can buy.

### Stage 3: Teaching and staff pay

11. **Examination & Results:** Needs subjects, students and attendance for the report card.
12. **Homework & Learning:** Needs classes, subjects and teacher allocation.
13. **Salary Management:** Needs staff, staff attendance and leave, and posts into the Day Book.
14. **Front Office:** Hands enquiries to Admissions and checks pickups against student records.
15. **Events & Calendar:** PTMs need results and teacher slots; event payments use Fee Management.
16. **Reports & Analytics:** Brings together the reports of every module built so far.

### Stage 4: Services a school can switch on

17. **Transport Management:** Needs students and fees for stop-wise transport fees; most asked for, so first here.
18. **Library Management:** Needs students, staff and ID cards.
19. **Inventory Management:** Needs Finance for purchases and bills.
20. **Hostel Management:** Needs students, fees and attendance; fewest schools use it, so last.

## 8. Not included

- **Safety & Health:** Health records, medical room, vaccinations, safety incidents and the nurse role.
- **Parent Portal / parent app:** No parent login. Parents still get WhatsApp, SMS and email messages, and fee payment links.
- **School Website / CMS:** Website pages, news and online admission form on the website.
- **Super Admin console:** Creating school accounts, plans, billing, subscriptions, support tickets and system monitoring. This is a separate product for DREAM TEAM SERVICES.
- **"Share the App" link:** Removed from the menu; it is not school work.

## 9. Questions for you

1. **Which online payment gateway should we connect first: Razorpay, PhonePe or Easebuzz?**  
   My recommendation: **Razorpay**. It is already active in the demo school and most Indian schools know it. The others follow one by one. Cash and UPI at the counter work from the first day.
2. **Should staff be allowed to delete a fee payment? Yes or No.**  
   My recommendation: **No**. A wrong payment is cancelled with a reason and stays on record, so receipts, balances and the Day Book always match. The old software allowed deleting.
3. **Should Transport, with live bus tracking, move into the first sellable version? Yes or No.**  
   My recommendation: **No**. The first version reaches schools sooner without it. Transport is the first module built after the rest, and not every school runs buses.

Every feature of the old software and the requirement documents has a place in this plan. The full list is in the feature map appendix.
