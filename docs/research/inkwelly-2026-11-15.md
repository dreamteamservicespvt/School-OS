## 1. The page in short

- Tabs "Fees · Attendance · Exams & report cards · Parent messages · Transport" with one short story video each. Below the text, a "Next: <feature>" button moves to the next tab. On a phone the tabs are a horizontal chip row, and the video sits under the words.

A fake cursor taps buttons with a ripple. Numbers count up. Rings fill. Success gets a full-screen green circle wipe and a little confetti.

- Pricing per student per year (Lite / Standard / Premium), a cost estimator, a "switching is on us" block, testimonials, FAQ.

4. **Each search result carries the due chip.** The cashier knows before opening the student.
5. **Installment stepper with one action per row** (View / Collect / Generate) and "₹X left" in red.
6. **Full / ½ / ¼ amount chips** on Collect and "Settles the invoice in full" as live feedback.
7. **Cashier PIN before recording cash.** A real money-safety idea for a shared counter computer (see adopt list, owner decision).
8. **Invoice PDF with UPI QR, sent in bulk, with a live "parents notified" count.**
9. The parent's "You've saved ₹1,950" (concession shown kindly) and the "80% paid" ring.

Better than ours or missing in ours:

3. **Approved leave shown inside the row** with dates and who approved. We already fill reasons from approved leave (`src/lib/attendance/absence-reasons.ts`); the row should say it, so the teacher does not overwrite it.

Better than ours or missing in ours (our plan: `School-OS-assistant/docs/assistant/plan.md`, panel `src/components/assistant/assistant-panel.tsx`):
1. **Conversation history** grouped Today / Yesterday with auto titles. Our panel keeps one `conversationId` and no list of past chats.
2. **One quiet "Used N tools ›" line** (live while working, collapsible after). It shows the work without noise and lets a careful principal open the details. Our plan has "sources" and an "Open the full screen" link, not a work line.
3. **Answer = one plain sentence with bold numbers + KPI tiles with comparisons** ("+12% vs usual"). Our plan has "one short spoken line and a card"; the tile row with a comparison is a concrete, proven shape for it.
4. **Approval card details we lack**: the question-form title, "Nothing changes until you approve", an **expiry** on the prepared change, a stays-in-the-chat "Approved · <action>" chip, and a "Done." line that names the new record number and every side effect (invoice created, welcome sent). Our enquiry card shows every field and writes "prepared by AI, confirmed by <person>", which is stronger on audit; it lacks the expiry, the persistent chip and the side-effects line.
5. **Files as answers** (Excel in a side panel with Download).
6. **Cost per answer** ("3 credits") and the balance under the user's name. We log every AI Call with cost in paise and have a daily limit, but the person never sees it.
7. **Stop button** while the AI works.

Where we are already better or must stay different:
- **Telugu.** They promise Hindi, English and Hinglish only. Ours follows the person in English, Telugu or Hindi, including romanised Telugu ("ee roju absent evaru?"). Say this loudly in our own marketing.
- **The number guard** (`numbers.ts`: a reply with a number not in the records is never shown). Their demo answers "12% more than a usual Tuesday" with no visible source.
- **Money and bulk messages.** Their "Send reminder to 22 parents? Approve" lets a chat card send bulk messages. Our blueprint (§2.1 rule 5, plan §2) says messages to many people go through the normal preview. A card may be allowed only if it IS that preview (count, opted-out count, template text, credit cost, the "Send to 22 parents" wording of P4). Owner decision.
- **"AI Connect · MCP · Works in Claude & ChatGPT"**: school data, including children's records and parent phones, leaving to outside chat apps. Not for us without a DPDP review, per-school consent and a read-only, field-limited scope.

## 4. What not to copy (risky or misleading)

1. **Countdown timer "Diwali offer · ends in 00 d 06 h 48 m".** Checked at 11:41 UTC on 15 Nov from two time zones: both said the same, so it ends at midnight India time; a week after Diwali with "0 days" left, it looks like a deadline that rolls each night (not proven; it would need a check after midnight). Fake urgency is a dark pattern; India's CCPA Guidelines for Prevention and Regulation of Dark Patterns (2023) list "false urgency". Never on our site.
2. **"4.9 from 5,000+ school members"** with no source, and testimonials with no person's name, only a school and a role. Our rule: real numbers and real, named, consenting people only, or a marked placeholder.
3. **"India's smartest school AI"**: an unprovable superlative.
4. **"Powered by <model brand>" and "Works in Claude & ChatGPT"** with the vendors' logos: needs the vendors' brand permission and is only true while that contract lasts. If we say which model we use, say it plainly in the privacy page, not as a badge.
5. **"Never used for training"**: only claim it if every AI provider's contract for our account guarantees it, in writing.
6. **"Go live the next working day", "₹0 migration", "Reports ready in 3 seconds", "Done in 40 seconds"**: promises that depend on the school's data; say what we have measured, with the conditions.
7. **AI-generated people presented as real teachers and parents** in the videos. If we make such videos, use real staff with consent, or label them.
8. **The AI sending bulk messages from a chat card** and **admitting a student in one approve tap** (with the fee plan): our blueprint keeps bulk messages and money behind the normal preview; any change here is an owner decision, not a copy.
9. **Class rank and a top-3 podium with children's photos** for parents.
10. **"Rate us" stars on the payment success screen.**
11. **Stories that auto-play with no pause, no captions**: fails WCAG 2.2.2 (moving content over 5 s needs a pause) for our own marketing.
12. **The phone view of the AI demo** is a shrunken desktop picture; if we show our assistant on a phone, show the real phone layout.
