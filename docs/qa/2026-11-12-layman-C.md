## Problems

### LC1 (High): a typed "Publish at" date is silently thrown away, the notice goes out at once
- Steps: Admin, Communication, New notice. Fill title and text, tick Class 1 A. In "Publish at" type 20-11-2026 in the date box and leave the time box (it shows "9:30 AM" in grey). Save draft (or Send for approval). Open Edit draft again.
- What happened: the date is gone. The notice page says "Published: Not yet" with no planned date, and Edit shows "Publish at" empty. Sent for approval, it would publish the moment the Principal approves, not on 20-11. No message said the time was missing. The grey "9:30 AM" looks like a filled-in value, so a clerk thinks it is set. Confirmed twice (two notices).
- Should: either use 9:30 AM (the shown default) or stop with "Choose a time for 20-11-2026". Never drop a date the person typed.
- Screenshot: `qa-output/layman-c/c-notice-dateonly-view.png` (date filled, time grey), `c-notice-expiry.png` (saved with no publish date).
- Likely source: `src/components/ui/date-input.tsx` `DateTimeInput` (`join()` returns "" unless both date and time are set); `src/app/(school)/communication/notices/notice-form.tsx`. Same box is used for "Send at" on `communication/parent-link/send` and other schedule fields; check them all.

- Screenshot: `qa-output/layman-c/c-hw-bad-after.png` (long "Sending…").
- Likely source: `src/app/(school)/homework-learning/actions.ts` (give homework action and the credit check inside the transaction).
