## Problems

### LE1 (Medium) Add item forgets the Category and Opening stock after any mistake
- Steps: Store keeper, Inventory Management, Items & Stock, Add item. Type a name, choose Category "Stationery", Reorder level -5, Price 12.50, Opening stock 10. Press Add item.
- What happened: the error "Reorder level cannot be less than 0." shows, but Category jumps back to "Choose the category" and the Opening stock box is empty again. Name, reorder and price are kept. The hint under Reorder level still says "the category's (20)" for a category that is no longer chosen. If the person fixes only the red field and saves again, it fails again on the category; if they do not notice the stock box, the item is added with no stock.
- Should: every value typed stays after an error.
- Screenshots: `qa-output/layman-e/inv2-01-reorder-negative.png`, `qa-output/layman-e/inv-03-saved-dblclick.png`
- Likely source: `src/app/(school)/inventory-management/_components/sheets.tsx` (ItemSheet: the category `<select key={cat.key}>` resets and `open-qty-*` inputs have no saved value).
- Re-checked: yes, 2 runs.

### LE2 (Medium) A book page and an item page never show the book's or item's name
- Steps: Library Management, Books, open any book (for example the new "LE-అగ్ని సిరకలు ..."). Or Inventory Management, Items & Stock, open any item.
- What happened: the big heading is "Library Management" / "Inventory Management" and the line under it is only "Books" or "Items & Stock · ST-0039". The name appears only in the browser tab; for a book the title is nowhere on the page (the author is only in the Details box). A librarian cannot tell which book she opened before she withdraws a copy or marks it lost.
- Should: the heading (or the line under it) shows the book title / item name.
- Screenshots: `qa-output/layman-e/lib2-04-book-page.png`, `qa-output/layman-e/inv3-01-item.png`
- Likely source: `src/components/shell/module-head.tsx` shows only `title`; `pageName` (passed as the record name by `inventory-management/items/[id]/page.tsx` line 93 and the library book page) is used only for the pin label. Probably the same on asset and purchase order pages.
- Re-checked: yes (book and item).

### LE3 (Medium) Issue desk: a wrongly typed due date is silently replaced by the normal due date
- Steps: Librarian, Issue & Return. Card SPS/2023/0055, book "Ashoka", Issue copy. In Due date paste or type 2026-12-31 (the box shows "20-26-12"). Press Issue: the box turns red ("Type the date as DD-MM-YYYY") and "Why a different due date?" appears. Type a reason, press Issue.
- What happened: the book is issued with the normal due date 26-11-2026, while the due box still shows "20-26-12" and a reason for a different date was given. The librarian believes she gave a later date. Typing 31122026 digit by digit works correctly (due 31-12-2026).
- Should: refuse again until the date is right, or ask; never issue with a different date than the one on screen. Also pasting 2026-12-31 should become 31-12-2026, not 20-26-12. The example "14-05-2012" is a past date; use a future one here.
- Screenshots: `qa-output/layman-e/desk2-12-issue-no-reason.png`, `qa-output/layman-e/desk4-1.png`, `qa-output/layman-e/desk4-2.png`
- Likely source: `src/app/(school)/library-management/_components/desk-forms.tsx` (IssueForm, DateInput) and the issue action in `src/app/(school)/library-management/actions.ts`.
- Re-checked: yes (2 runs). Both test copies were returned.

### LE4 (Medium) Invite a user: "Teacher" is already ticked, so every invited person also gets Teacher access
- Steps: Admin, Settings, Users & Permissions, Invite a user. Fill name, username, phone, tick "Librarian", Send invite. The new person signs in.
- What happened: My profile shows "Teacher, Librarian". The Teacher role was pre-ticked on the form. Other testers' invites today ("QA Invite 57984", "QA Invite 91110") also ended up as "Accountant, Teacher". The form says a person gets the widest access of all their roles, so this gives extra access nobody asked for.
- Should: no role ticked by default (or only the one the admin picks); at least show clearly that Teacher is already ticked.
- Screenshots: `qa-output/layman-e/user-03-sent.png`, `qa-output/layman-e/pw-02-wrong-current.png`, `qa-output/layman-e/admin-settings-users-390.png`
- Likely source: `src/app/(school)/settings/users/new/page.tsx` line 28-42 (`defaultRoleId={teacher?.id}`).
- Re-checked: yes (code and three invited users).

### LE5 (Medium) Excel downloads hold every number and rupee amount as text
- Steps: Reports & Analytics, open "Stock in hand" (or Library fines, Books issued), Export, Excel. Open the file and try to add up the Value or Amount column.
- What happened: cells such as "55", "₹260", "₹14,300", "₹150" are text, not numbers, so SUM gives 0 and sorting by amount is wrong. Same in the page exports (Library catalogue, Members, Fines, Items).
- Should: quantities and money as numbers with a rupee number format, so the office can total them in Excel.
- Files: `qa-output/layman-e/downloads/inv-stock.xlsx`, `qa-output/layman-e/downloads/lib-fines.xlsx`
- Likely source: `src/lib/exports.ts`.
- Re-checked: yes (2 files, cell types read with exceljs).

### LE8 (Medium) Telugu and Hindi screens are still half English in Library and Inventory
- Steps: My profile, Language, Telugu (librarian), open the Library pages; Hindi (store keeper), open the Inventory pages. (Both were put back to English.)
- What happened: page titles and tabs are translated, but much of the working text is English: on the Issue desk "Issued today", "Returned today", "Due today", "Overdue", "Reservations", "Member", "Book", the scan box text "Scan the library card, or type a name or number", the help line, "since", the table headings; on Books, Members, Fines, Reports and Settings the stat cards, table headings, "Export", "Block", "All dates", the settings labels ("Books for Primary", "Loan days for Primary"). Counted lines that are fully English: Library desk 27 of 67, Books 62 of 139, Members 57 of 151, Fines 95 of 208, Reports 53 of 149, Settings 63 of 121; Inventory home 14 of 48, Items 58 of 171, Purchases 14 of 49, Reports 53 of 87, Settings 62 of 108 (this count includes names of books, items and people, which correctly stay as written).
- Should: a Telugu or Hindi librarian/store keeper can do the daily desk work without English. The profile does say "Words not yet translated show in English", so this is a gap list, not a crash.
- Screenshots: `qa-output/layman-e/lang-librarian-te-library-management.png`, other `qa-output/layman-e/lang-*.png`; English lines listed in `qa-output/layman-e/lang-librarian-te.txt` and `qa-output/layman-e/lang-store-hi.txt`.
- Likely source: `src/lib/i18n/catalogues/te.ts`, `hi.ts`, and strings in library/inventory pages not wrapped in `<Words>`/`t()`.

### LE9 (Observation, Medium if seen by a real school) Slow pages and one "School OS could not open" under several users
- With 5 to 6 testers working at once, many pages took 5 to 20 s (Inventory settings 15 s, Library books 17 s, Stock in hand report 21 s). Once, the librarian opening `/reports-analytics/r/inv-stock` got "School OS could not open. Something went wrong on our side." instead of the no-access page; on retry it showed the correct no-access page. The server log at that time shows `Transaction already closed ... timeout for this transaction was 15000 ms, however 17751 ms passed` (also 30000 ms on /dashboard and on notifications).
- Should: a school with a few people on at once must not get error pages; worth a look at long transactions (the dashboard and notifications ones too).
- Source: `tools/app.log` lines around 324-348; likely `withSchool()` transactions in `src/lib/db.ts` on heavy pages.
- Re-checked: the error page did not repeat (load related).
