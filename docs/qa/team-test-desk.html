On every screen, ask yourself 8 questions

  • Does the button do what its name says?
  • If I make a mistake, is the message clear?
  • After saving and refreshing, is the number or name still right?
  • Can I fix a mistake later (edit or cancel)?
  • Does it look right on my phone too?
  • Did it open in under 3 seconds?
  • Is there any word I don't understand?
  • Did the right person, and only the right person, see it?

"Break it" list (try these on purpose)

  • Press Save twice quickly. Press Back after saving.
  • Leave boxes empty; type letters in money or phone boxes.
  • Very long names, Telugu or Hindi names, an emoji.
  • Dates like 31-02, a past date, a future date.
  • Collect the same fee twice for the same student.
  • As a Teacher, paste the address of a Principal-only page.
  • Turn the phone's internet off in the middle of a save.
  • Upload a very big photo or a wrong file type.

Rules (read once)
  • Use only the Test School. Never a real school's data.
  • One person, one login. Never share a login, never send passwords in WhatsApp.
  • Online payments are in test mode. No real money moves. Never use your real card.
  • Records whose names start with LA-, LB-, LC-, LD- or LE- were made by a computer tester. Leave them alone and don't count them.
  • Write it down the moment you see it. Don't fix, don't argue, don't wait.
  • Nothing in School OS is deleted for good. Things are archived or cancelled with a reason, so a "delete" button you expected may be called Archive, Cancel or Change status.
