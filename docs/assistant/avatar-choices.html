The three looks

Show all as
Idle

Panel open, waiting. Breathes and blinks, so it never looks frozen.

Listening

The mic is on. Moves with your voice; the words appear as it hears them.

Thinking

Working on it. After a moment it says what it is looking at.

Speaking

Answering. Tap the mic to stop it and talk again.

Happy

A change you said yes to is saved. A short cheer, then back to idle.

Sorry

Not allowed, or it doesn't know. Gentle, and it offers the right screen.

What to look at

  1. The face at the top shows what it is doing: listening, thinking, speaking, done or sorry. The words beside it say the same thing.
  2. One big mic button. Tap, speak, done. While it speaks, the same button stops it.
  3. Suggestions in plain words for the person's role. One tap asks.
  4. Every answer is a card with the number, what was counted, and "Open the full screen".
  5. Changes ask first. The card lists exactly what will change. Nothing happens until "Yes, do it".
  6. Money is never moved by the assistant. It fills the form; you press Collect.

Why people can trust it

Four promises the design keeps

Reading is instant

Answers come from your school's own records, only those your role may see. Every number opens the list behind it.

Changes ask first

Any change shows a card with exactly what will change. Nothing happens until you tap "Yes, do it".

Money needs your own tap

For fees, refunds and salary it only fills the form. You press the button on the normal screen. It never approves anything.

Never guesses

If it doesn't know, or your role can't do it, it says so and opens the right screen. Text inside records is never taken as an order.
